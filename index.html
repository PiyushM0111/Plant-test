⚠️ Danger: Urgent Action Needed 🍅 Tomato Plant

Leaf Blight (Fungus Attack)

Leaf Recovery Chance: 85% (High) Damage: 15%

What You Must Do (Step by Step):

✂️

Step 1: Cut Off Sick Leaves

Use clean scissors to cut off the yellow and spotted leaves at the bottom of the plant. Throw them in the trash. Do not leave them on the ground.

🧴

Step 2: Spray Home Neem Medicine

Mix 1 spoon Neem Oil + 1 Liter Water + 2 drops soap. Shake well in a spray bottle. Spray on top and underneath all leaves in the evening at sunset.

💧

Step 3: Water Soil, NOT Leaves

Pour water directly into the soil near the roots. Never splash water on leaves, because wet leaves make fungus grow fast.
